HomeHub · Music

A standalone Music app

Built on the Sonos + Spotify functionality already in Music.svelte — groups, transport, volume, favorites, search. New motif for this app specifically: a small live waveform marks anything actually playing, everywhere in the app, instead of a plain dot. Docked mini-player expands into a full player. Rooms is a tap-to-select grid, not a list.

Nav update — no persistent switcher. The pill subnav under the header is gone. Music's header now behaves like every other header in HomeHub: nothing rides below it but content. Search moves behind a plain search icon (it pushes in like Search everywhere else); Rooms is already one tap away from the room chips and "Manage" link on Home, so it doesn't need a permanent tab either. One less bar, one less thing to explain.

Two more fixes. Grouping was called "Rooms" here too, colliding with the app-level Rooms destination — renamed to Zones. And a hard rule going forward: a sheet never opens another sheet. Search, Zones and the full player are all one level deep off Home, so they can stay sheets. Speakers can't — its rows open a detail screen — so it's a real screen with its own back chevron instead.
